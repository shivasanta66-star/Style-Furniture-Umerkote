A shop on the main road, and the people in it

Style Furniture has been furnishing homes in Umerkote and the villages around it since 2012 — one delivery, one customer and a good deal of word of mouth at a time.

Our story

Started small. Still answering the same phone.

We began with a single room of beds and steel almirahs, and a van that was borrowed more often than not. What kept us going was simple: people in Umerkote wanted to see and touch furniture before paying for it, and they wanted someone local to call when something needed fixing.

That has not changed. The showroom is bigger now and the range runs from mattresses to full bedroom sets, but it is still a family business. The person who quotes you the price is the person who will answer when you ring back.

  • A decade-plus serving Umerkote, Nabarangpur and the surrounding blocks.
  • Our own delivery team and carpenters — no third-party handover.
  • Made-to-order work for rooms that standard sizes do not suit.

How we work

Four things we will not cut corners on

Say what it is

If it is MDF, we call it MDF. You should know what you are paying for and how long it will last.

Quote the whole price

Delivery, assembly and taxes on the quote from the start. No number that grows on the day.

Fit it properly

Levelled, tightened, wall-fixed where it needs to be, and the packing carried away.

Pick up the phone

After the sale is when it counts. Warranty claims and small repairs get handled, not deflected.

The team

Carpenters, fitters, and one very patient delivery van

Between the showroom floor, the workshop and the road, a small team covers the whole district. They measure, they build, they carry things up narrow staircases, and they come back when a joint needs tightening.

If you are furnishing a whole house, ask for a home visit — it is free, and it saves everyone a wasted delivery.
