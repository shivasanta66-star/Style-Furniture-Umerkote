Why buy here

Bought in person, backed in person

Furniture bought online arrives flat, in a box, with a phone number that rings somewhere far away. We are on the main road, and we are still here when a hinge loosens two years later.

  • Try before you buy. Sit, lie down, open every drawer. Nothing in the showroom is off limits.
  • Made to your size. A wardrobe for an awkward wall, a bed for a small room — we will get it built.
  • Honest materials. We will tell you plainly what is solid wood, what is ply and what is MDF, and what that means for the price.
  • After-sales that answers. One number, one shop, and a team that comes back out.
