A mattress is the one thing people regret buying in a hurry. Here is how we help you choose, in plain words.

Orthopedic, firm

For back or waist pain, and for anyone who wakes up stiff. The body stays straight; the mattress does not sink in the middle.

Best for: elders, back pain, heavier build

Medium

The safe choice when two people share the bed and want different things. Firm underneath, a little give on top.

Best for: couples, daily family use

Soft

For a light body and for side sleepers, or a guest room where the bed is used now and then.

Best for: children, light build, guest rooms
