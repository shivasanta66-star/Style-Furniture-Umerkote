# Google Business Profile checklist: Style Furniture, Umerkote

The website can only do half of local search. The other half is the Google
Business Profile (the listing that shows on Google Maps and in "furniture shop
near me" searches). These steps are done in the Profile, not in this code.
Keep the shop name, address and phone number **identical** everywhere.

## Set up
1. **Primary category:** Furniture store. **Additional:** Sofa store, Mattress store, Bed shop, Water purifier supplier (only the ones that are true).
2. **Services / products:** add each product group (3+1+1 sofa set, queen bed, orthopedic mattress, dining set, steel almirah, water purifier) with a short description.
3. **Attributes:** turn on any that apply (e.g. Delivery, On-site services) once the owner confirms them.
4. **Appointment / Message links:** add WhatsApp (`https://wa.me/919937601505`).
5. **Description (750 characters):** what you sell, where you are (opposite the Block Office), which villages you deliver to, and that you are closed Wednesday.

## Photos: the biggest gain
Add at least 10 real photos: shop front with the signboard, inside the showroom,
each product group, the Block Office landmark next to the shop. Add 2–3 new
photos every month. Real photos also replace the illustrations on the website.

## Reviews
- Reply to every review, including the existing 26, in a sentence or two.
- Ask happy customers in person to review, and give them the listing link
  `https://maps.app.goo.gl/Ej5kVxfLn61TXxsT6`. Do not offer discounts or gifts for reviews.

## Keep it fresh
- Post an update every 1–2 weeks (new arrival, festival offer, a delivery photo).
- Update hours for festivals.
- Answer questions that appear under "Questions & answers", and add the common ones yourself (closed Wednesday, delivery area, fitting at home).

## After the site is live
- Add the site in Google Search Console and submit `https://stylefurnitureumerkote.netlify.app/sitemap.xml`.
- Test the page at https://search.google.com/test/rich-results to confirm the structured data is read.
- Netlify's `netlify.app` address is fine to start. If the shop buys its own domain, update the canonical, `og:url`, sitemap and JSON-LD `url`.
