Before you buy

Questions we get asked most

Do you deliver outside Umerkote town?

Yes. Delivery inside Umerkote town is free. For the surrounding villages and elsewhere in Nabarangpur district we charge a flat rate by distance — ask us for the exact figure when you confirm the order.

Can I get something made to my own size?

Most beds, wardrobes and kitchen units can be built to size. Bring your measurements, or we will come and measure the room. Made-to-order work usually takes two to three weeks.

What is the difference between solid wood, ply and MDF?

Solid wood (sheesham, teak) lasts longest and costs most. Plywood is strong, handles humidity well and sits in the middle. MDF is the most affordable and is fine for light-use furniture kept dry. We will tell you which one you are looking at, every time.

Is there a warranty?

Every piece carries the manufacturer warranty, printed on your bill. If something goes wrong inside that period, bring us the bill — we raise the claim and handle the repair or replacement for you.

Do you take EMI or card payment?

Cash, UPI, debit and credit cards, and card EMI on larger purchases. For bulk or made-to-order orders we also take staged payment.

Will you take my old furniture?

On some purchases we can arrange an exchange against your old set. It depends on the condition, so send us a photo on WhatsApp first.
