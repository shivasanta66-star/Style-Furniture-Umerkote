Showroom photographs go here.

Reference them from `<image-slot src="assets/img/...">` in the pages, or from the
`image` field of an entry in `assets/js/products.js`. Until then each slot draws
a labelled placeholder at the correct aspect ratio.

Suggested: `shopfront.jpg`, `floor-wide.jpg`, one shot per room category, and a
few delivered sets in customers' homes. Export at roughly 1600px on the long
edge and compress before committing.
